# Heyra email style

Heyra is a fictional Danish betting and gaming operator used in this demo.
Addresses below end in `heyra.example` and do not exist.

Answer in the language the user writes in. Draft emails in the language of
the thread.

## Greeting and sign-off

| Recipient              | Greeting (DA)          | Sign-off (DA)           | English                          |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Colleague              | Hej <fornavn>          | Vh / Hilsen <navn>      | Hi <name> / Best, <name>         |
| Retail partner         | Hej <fornavn>          | Venlig hilsen           | Hi <name> / Kind regards         |
| Supplier, agency       | Hej <fornavn>          | Venlig hilsen           | Hello <name> / Kind regards      |
| Authority, lawyer      | Kære <fulde navn>      | Med venlig hilsen       | Dear <full name> / Yours sincerely |
| Journalist             | Hej <fornavn>          | Venlig hilsen           | Hi <name> / Best regards         |

Always "du", never "De", except to an authority when the thread already
uses "De".

## Subject lines

Tag the subject when it helps the reader sort:

- `[Handling]` the reader must do something. Add the deadline: `[Handling, frist 20/9]`.
- `[Beslutning]` a decision is needed.
- `[Til info]` no action.
- Keep the original subject on replies. Never "Re: Re: Re:"; Outlook handles it.

## Response times (house rule)

| Mail from                 | Reply within        |
| ------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Colleague with a question | 1 working day       |
| Retail partner            | 1 working day, redirect operational issues to Forhandlerservice |
| Authority                 | Same day: acknowledge, then answer within their deadline |
| Journalist                | Redirect to Presse the same hour |

## Tone

- Answer first. The first sentence contains the answer or the decision.
- Short paragraphs, one idea each. Bullets for three or more items.
- No hedging stacks. "Jeg anbefaler" instead of "jeg tænker måske at det kunne være en idé at".
- Name the next step and who owns it.
- Warm but not chatty. One friendly line at most.

## GDPR and player data (hard rules)

- Never put a CPR number, a player account number, a deposit amount or a
  self-exclusion status into an email. Refer to "the case" or use the case
  number from the customer system.
- Never forward a player's email outside Heyra.
- Attachments with player data go through the secure share in ~~files with
  access limited to the case team, never as an email attachment.
- If in doubt, write "Jeg ringer til dig om detaljerne" and call.

## Redirect, do not answer

| Mail type                                              | Send to                            | Then                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| A player writing about their account, a payout, a game | kundecenter@heyra.example          | Reply nothing about the case. One line: forwarded to the customer centre |
| A request to pause, set a limit or self-exclude        | ansvarligtspil@heyra.example       | **Same day.** No marketing content in the reply. Acknowledge that it is forwarded |
| A journalist                                           | presse@heyra.example               | Acknowledge, give the press phone number, no comment |
| A retailer about a terminal, stock or commission       | forhandlerservice@heyra.example    | Acknowledge, forward                          |
| An invoice or payment reminder                         | faktura@heyra.example              | Forward, no reply needed                      |
| A job applicant                                        | job@heyra.example                  | Forward, one polite line                      |
| Anything that mentions fraud, money laundering, threats | compliance@heyra.example, and call | Do not reply                                  |

Examples of replies and redirects, in Danish and English, are in
[`references/examples.md`](references/examples.md).
