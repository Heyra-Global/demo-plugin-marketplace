# Heyra employee handbook (fictional, demo extract)

Version 2026-09. Invented for this demo. Danish terms in brackets.

## §1 Working hours (arbejdstid)

Full time is 37 hours per week. Core hours 09:30 to 15:00 at the head
office. The customer centre runs shifts between 10:00 and 20:00 every day;
shift plans are published four weeks ahead. Flexitime balance may vary
between minus 10 and plus 20 hours.

## §2 Remote work (hjemmearbejde)

Up to two days per week by agreement with the manager; customer-centre
staff by rota. Work from abroad requires HR approval and is limited to 20
days per year within the EU. Equipment allowance: see the expense policy.

## §3 Holidays and leave (ferie og orlov)

Five weeks of holiday under the Holiday Act (ferieloven) plus five extra
days (feriefridage) per year. Book in the HR system; the manager confirms
within five working days. At least three consecutive weeks may be taken in
the summer period. Parental leave follows the law and the collective
agreement; Heyra pays full salary for 24 weeks in total per child, split
by agreement. Care days (omsorgsdage): two per child per year for children
under 8.

## §4 Sickness (sygdom)

Report before 09:00 to the manager on the first day. A doctor's note may be
requested from day 4. Return-to-work conversation after 14 days of absence.
Long-term illness: HR contacts the employee within four weeks.

## §5 IT and phone use

Company devices are for work; limited private use is accepted. Never
install software outside the company store. Report lost devices to IT
within one hour. Player data is accessed only through the customer system,
never exported to mail or local files. Phishing: report, do not click.

## §6 Employees and Heyra's games (medarbejdere og spil)

Employees and members of their household may not play Heyra Odds or Heyra
Kasino. Draw games (Ugens Tal, Nordtal), scratch cards and bingo are
allowed on the same terms as any player, within a personal deposit limit.
Employees with access to draw systems, prize data or the customer system
may not play any Heyra game. Employee winnings above DKK 1,000 are reported
to Compliance for a routine check. Company money is never used for play.

## §7 Conflicts of interest (habilitet)

Declare in the HR system any ownership, board seat or close relation
concerning a retailer, supplier, sponsored club or competitor. Do not take
part in decisions about them. Gifts: see the expense policy.

## §8 Responsible gaming duty

Every employee who notices signs of problem gambling in a player, a
retailer's customer or a colleague reports it to Ansvarligt Spil. There is
no wrong report. Marketing, product and customer-centre staff complete the
yearly responsible-gaming training.

## §9 Whistleblowing (whistleblowerordning)

Report suspected breaches of law or serious misconduct through the
whistleblower portal (anonymous option) or to the external lawyer named on
the portal. Retaliation is a dismissal offence.

## §10 Confidentiality and press

Draw results, jackpot amounts before publication, winner identities and
regulator correspondence are confidential. Only Presse speaks to media.

## §11 Dress, alcohol, events

No dress code beyond "meet the customer as you would like to be met".
Alcohol only at company events after work hours, never during customer-
centre shifts or retailer visits.

## §12 Contacts

HR & People: hr@heyra.example. Ansvarligt Spil: ansvarligtspil@heyra.example.
Compliance: compliance@heyra.example. IT & Digital: it@heyra.example.
