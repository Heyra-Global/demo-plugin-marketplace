# GDPR request procedure (fictional Heyra extract)

Demo material. Real procedures are owned by the legal team.

## Request types

| Type          | Danish                    | Article | What the person gets                                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Access        | indsigt                   | 15      | A copy of their data, purposes, recipients, retention, sources     |
| Rectification | berigtigelse              | 16      | Correction of wrong data                                          |
| Erasure       | sletning                  | 17      | Deletion, except where a legal duty to keep applies               |
| Restriction   | begrænsning               | 18      | Data kept but not used while a dispute is resolved                |
| Portability   | dataportabilitet          | 20      | Their provided data in a machine-readable file                    |
| Objection     | indsigelse                | 21      | Stop of processing based on legitimate interest, e.g. marketing   |

Deadline: one month from receipt, extendable by two months for complex or
many requests; the person is told within the first month.

## Systems by type of person

| Person           | Systems                                                                 | Owner                 |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Player           | Customer system, payment system, responsible-gaming case log, marketing consent store, mail to Kundecenter | Kundecenter, IT & Digital, Ansvarligt Spil |
| Retailer contact | CRM, retailer portal, contract archive, mail                            | Partnerskaber, Jura   |
| Employee         | HR system, payroll, IT logs, mail                                       | HR & People, IT       |
| Applicant        | Recruiting system, mail                                                 | HR & People           |

## Retention exceptions (what is kept even after an erasure request)

| Record                                     | Kept for                                    | Basis                                  |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Customer due diligence and AML records     | 5 years after the relationship ends         | Anti-money-laundering act              |
| Accounting records incl. payouts           | 5 years from the end of the financial year  | Bookkeeping act                        |
| Self-exclusion and limit history           | As required by the Danish Gambling Authority | Gambling act and executive orders     |
| Records needed for a legal claim           | Until the claim is settled                  | Legitimate interest                    |

Everything else about a player is erased or anonymised within 30 days of
the decision.

## Reply templates

### Verification request (DA)

```
Hej

Tak for din henvendelse. For at beskytte dine oplysninger skal vi
bekræfte, at du er den person, anmodningen handler om. Log ind på din
konto med MitID og send anmodningen derfra, eller udfyld og underskriv
den vedhæftede formular.

Fristen på en måned løber fra i dag, <dato>.

Venlig hilsen
Jura & GDPR, Heyra
```

### Access reply (DA)

```
Hej <fornavn>

Vedlagt finder du en kopi af de oplysninger, vi behandler om dig, samt en
oversigt over formål, modtagere og opbevaringstid. Oplysningerne stammer
fra <kilder>.

Har du spørgsmål, eller mener du, at noget er forkert, kan du svare på
denne mail. Du kan også klage til Datatilsynet.

Venlig hilsen
Jura & GDPR, Heyra
```

### Erasure reply, partial (EN)

```
Dear <name>

We have deleted or anonymised the personal data we held about you, with
the following exceptions that we are required by law to keep:

- <record>, kept until <date>, under <act>.

These records are stored with restricted access and are not used for any
other purpose. You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency
(Datatilsynet) if you disagree with this decision.

Kind regards
Legal & GDPR, Heyra
```
