# Travel, expense and gifts policy (fictional)

Heyra is a fictional Danish betting and gaming operator. This policy is
invented for the demo. Answer in the user's language; quote the limit and
the rule, then say what the user should do.

## Principles

1. Spend as if it were your own money, and document it as if it were
   someone else's.
2. Every expense has a receipt, a business purpose and, for meals, the
   names of the people present.
3. Submit within 30 days in ~~finance. Later claims need department-head
   approval.

## Limits (DKK, incl. VAT)

| Category                         | Limit                         | Note                                        |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Hotel, Denmark                   | 1,400 per night               | Breakfast included where possible           |
| Hotel, abroad                    | 1,900 per night               | Capital cities: 2,300                       |
| Meals when travelling            | 450 per day                   | No alcohol reimbursed on travel days        |
| Team meals (internal)            | 350 per person                | Max four per year per team                  |
| Mileage, own car                 | State rate                    | Log start, end, purpose                     |
| Train, Denmark                   | Standard class                | Flexible ticket allowed for same-day return |
| Flights, Europe                  | Economy                       | Book at least 14 days ahead when possible   |
| Taxi                             | Only when public transport is unreasonable | Receipt with route              |
| Phone and internet, home         | 250 per month                 | Managers and on-call staff                  |
| Working from home equipment      | 3,000 per year                | Screen, chair, keyboard; company property   |

## Gifts and hospitality

| Situation                                   | Rule                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gift to a partner or retailer               | Max 1,000 per person per year, never cash, gift cards or gambling credit |
| Gift received from a supplier               | Max 500; above that, decline or hand to HR for a raffle            |
| Dinner with a partner                       | Max 600 per person, business purpose noted, no partner staff under 18 |
| Hospitality at Heyra-sponsored events       | Within the sponsorship package; guest list logged                   |
| Public officials, regulator staff           | Nothing. Coffee at a meeting is fine                               |

## Heyra games

- Company money is never used to buy or play Heyra games, including
  "testing" with a company card. Product testing uses test accounts issued
  by IT & Digital.
- Employees and their household may not play Heyra Odds or Heyra Kasino.
  Draw games and scratch cards are allowed within the same limits as any
  player. Winnings by employees follow the normal rules and the winner-
  advice process.

## Approval

| Amount (DKK)  | Approver          |
| ------------- | ----------------- |
| up to 5,000   | Team lead         |
| 5,001 to 25,000 | Department head |
| above 25,000  | CFO               |

Your own manager approves your expenses; nobody approves their own.

## How to answer a question

1. Find the category and the limit.
2. State yes, no, or "yes with approval by <role>".
3. Say what to attach and where to submit (~~finance, within 30 days).
4. If the policy does not cover it, say so and point to Økonomi.
