# Heyra partner playbook

Heyra is a fictional Danish betting and gaming operator invented for this
demo. Every figure below is made up.

Answer in the language the user writes in. Offers and mails to Danish
partners are written in Danish.

## Partner types

| Type          | Who                                          | What they get                                     | What Heyra gets                        |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Retail        | Kiosks, petrol stations, supermarkets, chains | Terminal, scratch-card stock, commission, training | Counter sales, presence                |
| Sponsorship   | Sports clubs, associations, events           | Funding, visibility on Heyra channels             | Brand association, hospitality         |
| B2B           | Employers, unions, member organisations      | Puljespil (syndicate) for their members, responsibly framed | Reach to adult members          |

## Retail tiers and commission (fictional)

| Tier        | Criteria                          | Commission on draws | Commission on scratch | Support             |
| ----------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------- |
| Kiosk       | Single outlet                     | 5.0 %               | 7.0 %                 | Phone, 08-16        |
| Supermarked | Single store above DKK 2 m yearly | 5.5 %               | 7.5 %                 | Named contact       |
| Kæde        | Chain agreement, 10+ outlets      | negotiated, 5.5 to 6.5 % | 7.5 to 8.5 %     | Key account manager |

Commission is the only money that flows to a retailer. No signing bonuses,
no volume prizes for staff, no free plays.

## Sponsorship packages (fictional)

| Package | Yearly fee (DKK) | Includes                                                    |
| ------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bronze  | 50,000           | Logo on club site, one Heyra post per season                |
| Sølv    | 200,000          | Bronze plus stadium logo (static, no odds), youth-free zones |
| Guld    | 750,000          | Sølv plus hospitality, co-branded responsible-gaming campaign |

Sponsorship never includes: live odds on club channels, player-facing
offers through the club, or activities aimed at members under 18.

## What Heyra may and may not offer partners

| May                                                      | May not                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Commission, training, point-of-sale material with the block | Bonuses or free plays to the partner's customers               |
| Co-branded responsible-gaming material                    | Odds or jackpot amounts on youth-facing surfaces                 |
| Hospitality within the gifts policy (DKK 1,000 per person per year) | Cash, gift cards or gambling credit as gifts             |
| Data on aggregate sales in the partner's outlets           | Any player-level data                                            |

Every retail partner completes Heyra's compliance training (age checks,
AML basics, responsible gaming) before the terminal is activated, and
yearly after that. Write it into every offer.

## Pipeline stages

Lead -> Kontakt -> Møde -> Tilbud -> Forhandling -> Aftale -> Onboarding

| Stage        | Exit criterion                                            |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lead         | Company, decision maker and fit noted in ~~CRM            |
| Kontakt      | A reply from the decision maker                           |
| Møde         | Meeting held, needs and objections logged                 |
| Tilbud       | Offer sent from the template, valid 30 days               |
| Forhandling  | Written counter-proposal received                         |
| Aftale       | Signed, compliance training scheduled                     |
| Onboarding   | Terminal live or sponsorship assets delivered              |

## CRM hygiene

- One deal per agreement. Amount = expected yearly commission or fee.
- Next step and date on every open deal. A deal with no next step is stale.
- Close date is the date the partner will sign, not the quarter end.
- Notes after every meeting, within the same day (use follow-up-email).

## Objections and answers

| Objection                                      | Answer                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Gambling does not fit our brand"              | Heyra's sponsorships are built around responsible-gaming messaging; show the Guld campaign |
| "The commission is too low"                    | Compare with footfall and basket size in similar outlets; commission is fixed by tier, extras are not possible |
| "Too much compliance work"                     | Training is 45 minutes online; Heyra handles age-check signage and AML reporting |
| "We want an exclusive"                         | Heyra does not grant exclusivity; offer the Kæde tier and a named key account manager |
