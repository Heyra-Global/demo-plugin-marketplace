# Danish gambling-marketing checklist (demo)

Loaded on demand by the compliance-check skill. This is a demo summary
written in September 2026 for a fictional operator. Rules change; the
compliance team owns the real list. Sources are given so a reader can verify.

Status: **in force** unless marked **pending**.

## A. Duty of disclosure (every ad that promotes gambling)

| #  | Rule                                                                                                  | How to check                                              | Source |
| -- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| A1 | Age limit stated. "18+" is sufficient for all Heyra games                                             | Look for "18+" in text or image                           | Danish Gambling Authority, guide on the duty of disclosure v3.0 (July 2025) |
| A2 | Reference to the StopSpillet helpline, presented as the authority's service, not Heyra's              | "StopSpillet" plus the number or a link                   | same |
| A3 | Reference to self-exclusion through ROFUS; a logo alone is not enough, logo plus text is              | "ROFUS" in text                                           | same |
| A4 | The regulator's label where the medium allows it (not radio, not SMS)                                  | "Spillemyndigheden" label present on visual media          | same |
| A5 | Applies to all media: print, digital, social posts and profiles, banners, SMS, audio, TV, cinema, outdoor, merchandise | Do not exempt "just a story" or "just a banner" | same |
| A6 | The website carries direct links to ROFUS and StopSpillet                                              | Web pages: footer links present                            | same |

Sources: spillemyndigheden.dk, "Vejledning om oplysningspligt ved
markedsføring af spil", version 3.0, 15 July 2025; logo guidelines for
StopSpillet and ROFUS on the same site.

## B. Content rules (Gambling Act section 36)

| #  | Rule                                                                              | How to check                                               |
| -- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| B1 | Present the chance of winning correctly and in balance; never overstate it         | Odds stated exactly or not at all; no "high chance"        |
| B2 | Present gambling as entertainment, not as income or investment                     | No "tjen", "invester", "indtægt", "earn"                   |
| B3 | Do not target under-18s in content or media choice                                 | No youth channels, no youth-coded visuals or language      |
| B4 | No celebrities or well-known people implying that gambling caused their success    | Check named people and their role                          |
| B5 | Do not imply that gambling solves money problems or brings social acceptance       | No "løs dine problemer", "bliv populær", "nyt liv"         |

Source: Lov om spil (Gambling Act), section 36, consolidated act 2025.

## C. Offers, bonuses and promotions

| #  | Rule                                                                             | How to check                                              |
| -- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| C1 | Maximum value DKK 1,000 at the time it is granted                                 | Value in the offer                                        |
| C2 | Required deposit or stake to release the bonus equals 100 % of the bonus value    | Deposit requirement in the terms                          |
| C3 | Wagering requirement at most 10 times the bonus                                   | "Omsætningskrav" figure                                   |
| C4 | At least 60 days to meet the terms                                                | Deadline in the terms                                     |
| C5 | All material terms visible in the ad itself (wagering, deadline, max, game limits) | Terms in the same asset, not only behind a link          |
| C6 | Boosted odds and "game challenges" count as promotions and follow the same rules  | Treat "boost" like a bonus                                |
| C7 | "Gratis" or "free" only when nothing is required in return                        | Deposit required means not free                           |

Source: Danish Gambling Authority, "Vejledning om salgsfremmende
foranstaltninger", version 5.0, 15 July 2025.

## D. Direct marketing and player protection

| #  | Rule                                                                                                    | How to check                                       |
| -- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| D1 | ROFUS check before any direct marketing (email, SMS, phone); registered persons must receive nothing     | Scrub date within 24 h before send, owner named     |
| D2 | Email and SMS marketing only with prior consent (Marketing Practices Act)                               | Consent source and date on the list                |
| D3 | No marketing to self-excluded or paused players, ever                                                    | Segment excludes paused and self-excluded accounts |
| D4 | Deposit-limit reminders are welcome; never frame a limit as an obstacle                                  | Tone check                                         |

Sources: Danish Gambling Authority on ROFUS and "No thanks to gambling
ads"; decisions of Spilreklamenævnet (industry advertising board), e.g.
formal criticism for an SMS to a ROFUS-registered person (2023).
