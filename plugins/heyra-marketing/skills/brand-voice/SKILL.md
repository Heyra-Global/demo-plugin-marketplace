# Heyra brand voice

Heyra in this plugin is a **fictional** Danish licensed betting and gaming
operator, invented for this demo. Every fact below is made up. The structure
is what a real brand skill looks like.

Answer in the language the user writes in. Examples are in Danish, with
English where useful.

## Heyra in one paragraph

Heyra A/S runs licensed games of chance in Denmark: the Saturday draw
**Ugens Tal**, the Wednesday draw **Nordtal**, **Skrabelykke** scratch cards,
the **Fast Spil** subscription, **Puljespil** syndicates, the **Heyra Odds**
sportsbook, **Heyra Kasino** and **Heyra Bingo**. About 320 people work at
the head office in Roskilde and in the customer centre. Around 2,800 kiosks
and supermarkets sell Heyra games at the counter. Heyra is licensed and
supervised by the Danish Gambling Authority (Spillemyndigheden).

**Tagline (DA):** _Spil med måde._ **Tagline (EN):** _Play for the fun of it._

**Mission:** Games that are fun to play, safe to play, and honest about the
odds.

## Tone of voice

| Do                                                        | Do not                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Warm, playful, plain Danish. "Du", never "De"             | Hype, pressure, countdown panic ("kun i dag!")            |
| The game is the fun. The prize is a possibility            | The prize is the reason. Money as the goal                 |
| State odds and prize amounts exactly, from the product team | Round up, imply, or hide the odds                        |
| One idea per sentence                                     | Long sentences with three commas                          |
| The responsible-gaming block on every player-facing piece | "Just this once without the 18+ line"                     |
| Respect the reader who does not play                      | Assume everyone wants to play                             |

Register by audience:

| Audience              | Register                                          |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Players (public)      | Warm, short, honest. Danish. Block required        |
| Retail partners       | Practical, respectful, compliance-aware            |
| Press                 | Neutral, factual, one spokesperson                 |
| Employees (Teams)     | Direct, friendly, no marketing voice               |
| Sponsors and B2B      | Professional, benefit-led, numbers with sources    |

## The mandatory block

Every player-facing piece that promotes a game ends with this block. It is
required by Danish marketing rules for gambling (see compliance-check for
the sources). Never shorten it.

```
18+ | Spil med måde | Hjælp: StopSpillet 70 22 28 25 | Udeluk dig selv via ROFUS | Spillemyndigheden
```

English version for international material:

```
18+ | Play for the fun of it | Help: StopSpillet +45 70 22 28 25 | Self-exclude via ROFUS | Licensed by the Danish Gambling Authority
```

Employer-brand and corporate posts that do not promote a game do not need
the block. When in doubt, add it.

## Words to avoid

Danish: garanteret, sikker gevinst, risikofrit, gratis penge, tjen penge,
invester, bliv rig, vind hver gang, din chance for et nyt liv, løs dine
pengeproblemer, "kun i dag", "sidste chance".

English: guaranteed, can't lose, risk-free, free money, earn, invest, get
rich, win every time, last chance.

Replacements: "chance for at vinde" (not "sikker gevinst"), "spil for sjov"
(not "tjen penge"), "puljen er på 30 mio. kr." (not "vind 30 millioner").

## Writing principles

1. **Lead with the game, not the money.** "Lørdag trækker vi Ugens Tal"
   before any amount.
2. **Numbers from the source.** Prize pools, odds and dates come from the
   product team or the ~~files campaign sheet. Never estimate.
3. **Short.** Posts under 80 words. Emails under 150. Press leads under 40.
4. **Honest about chance.** If you mention the pool, you may mention the
   odds. Never imply that playing more improves the odds of a draw.
5. **Include the non-player.** Winner stories celebrate the person, not the
   money. Ask for written consent before any winner is named.

## Colour palette

| Name    | Hex       | Role                                                     |
| ------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nordhav | `#10233F` | Primary dark. Headlines, dark backgrounds, wordmark       |
| Lys     | `#FBF7EF` | Warm light background                                    |
| Gevinst | `#F2B233` | Accent. One highlight per piece: the draw date, the CTA   |
| Hav     | `#1B7F8E` | Secondary. Links, sport, live elements                    |
| Sten    | `#6B7280` | Secondary text, captions, borders                        |
| Varsel  | `#D9483B` | Warnings and the responsible-gaming emphasis only        |

Rules: Nordhav text on Lys by default; Lys text on Nordhav for dark pieces.
Gevinst is an accent, never a text colour on Lys (too little contrast). The
responsible-gaming block is always set in Nordhav or Lys, never in Gevinst.

## Typography and logo

- Headings and body: Inter. Long-form winner stories: Georgia.
- Logo: [`assets/heyra-logo.svg`](assets/heyra-logo.svg). Clear space equal
  to the height of the H. Minimum width 96 px on screen, 25 mm in print.
  Never recolour, stretch or add effects. On photos, use a Nordhav panel.

## Checklist before anything ships

- [ ] First sentence names the game or the occasion, not the money.
- [ ] Every number has a source.
- [ ] No word from the avoid list.
- [ ] Mandatory block present and complete (player-facing).
- [ ] Gevinst used once, never for text on Lys.
- [ ] Winner named only with written consent.
- [ ] `compliance-check` run for anything paid, public or sent to players.
