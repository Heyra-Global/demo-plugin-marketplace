# LinkedIn post template (Nordlys)

```
<Hook: at most 12 words. A number, a contrast or a plain claim.>

<Paragraph 1: the situation the reader recognises. One or two sentences.>

<Paragraph 2: what changed, in plain words. Name the method only after the result.>

<Paragraph 3: the concrete result. "From X to Y in Z weeks." One number minimum.>

<Closing line: one question to the reader, or one named next step.>

#<Tag1> #<Tag2> #<Tag3>
```

## Example

```
Nine days to close the books. Four after twelve weeks.

Most manufacturers we meet trust their ERP and distrust their reports. The
numbers are right; they arrive too late to act on.

For a 400-person machinery maker we loaded ERP and MES data into Microsoft
Fabric every hour and built the three dashboards the plant managers had
asked for since 2023.

Month-end close went from 9 days to 4. The controller now opens one report,
not eleven spreadsheets.

Which report in your company do people rebuild by hand every month?

#DataPlatform #Manufacturing #MicrosoftFabric
```
