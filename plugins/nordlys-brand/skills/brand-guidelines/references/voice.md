# Nordlys voice: worked examples

This reference is loaded on demand. SKILL.md holds the rules; this file shows
them applied. Read it when you rewrite a draft and want a before/after pattern
to copy.

## Before and after

### Opening lines

Before:

> In today's fast-paced world, organisations need to leverage cutting-edge
> analytics to unlock the full potential of their data.

After:

> Most mid-size manufacturers close their books nine days after month end. Our
> last three clients now close in four.

### Product descriptions

Before:

> Our seamless, best-in-class platform empowers teams with robust, holistic
> insights.

After:

> We build the platform on Microsoft Fabric, load your ERP and MES data every
> hour, and hand you three dashboards your plant managers open every morning.

### Calls to action

Before:

> Reach out to learn how we can elevate your data journey.

After:

> Book a 30-minute call. Bring one report you do not trust. We will tell you
> what it would take to fix it.

## Audience registers

| Audience   | Lead with                              | Avoid                          |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| CTO        | Risk, cost, time to value, handover     | Feature lists                  |
| Data lead  | Architecture choices and trade-offs     | Vague "modern data stack" talk |
| Founder    | One number that changes a decision      | Platform vocabulary            |
| Plant/ops  | What they see at 07:00 tomorrow         | Anything about pipelines       |

## Structure of a LinkedIn post

1. Hook line, at most 12 words, carries a number or a tension.
2. Two to four short paragraphs. One idea each.
3. One concrete result with a number.
4. One question or one named next step.
5. At most three hashtags. At most one emoji, and only if the topic is light.

## Structure of a case study

1. Title: client type and the number that changed.
2. Situation in three sentences.
3. What we built, in the order the client experienced it.
4. Result table: metric, before, after.
5. What we would do differently.
